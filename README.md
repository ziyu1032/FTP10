# 🚀 Watch & Fill — Wenchang International Aerospace City

An English listening + gap-fill web game for **FTP Unit 3 · Warm-up** (Hainan Free Trade Port course).

**Play online:** https://ziyu1032.github.io/FTP10/

## How to use
1. **📺 Step 1 · Watch & Key Words** — watch the warm-up video, then tap each key word when you hear it (tap a card to hear it read aloud). English words with Chinese meanings are listed.
2. **✏️ Step 2 · Fill in the paragraph** — the whole passage with 10 blanks. Only the current blank is open: tap the correct word in the word bank. A wrong choice shakes and warns you at once — you must fill in the correct word before moving on. After 2 wrong tries a hint appears (first letter + Chinese meaning).
3. Finish all 10 to get the 🚀 Perfect! message. `🔁 Video` opens a mini player while filling; `🔄 Restart` starts over.

## Files
- `index.html` — the whole game (single file, no build step)
- `warm-up.mp4` — the warm-up video (compressed)
